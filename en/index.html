FAQ

What almost everyone asks us.

Have another question? Message us and we'll get back to you the same day.

Ask on WhatsApp →
How long does it take?

A Nexo assistant takes 2 to 3 weeks. A website, 3 to 4 weeks. Ad campaigns go live about a week after you give us access. Your plan includes the dates in writing.

Do I sign a contract?

Yes, always in writing: what's included, how fast we respond and what we deliver. Advertising is a 6-month agreement. Development work (websites, assistants, systems) is billed per project, with a deposit.

Is ad spend included?

No. You pay Meta or Google directly, from your own account and with your own card. We charge for management, strategy and reporting, so you always see where every dollar goes.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. We set everything up and train you and your team to use it. If anything is unclear, just message us.

What happens to my customers' data?

It's yours. We don't sell it or share it, and we only use it for the service you hired us for.

What does the assistant do if it doesn't know an answer?

It doesn't make things up. It hands the conversation to someone on your team and lets you know.

Do you work in English?

Yes. We work in English and Spanish, with businesses across the US and Mexico. Almost everything is done online, and we can meet in person when needed.

How much is the assessment?

Nothing. We review your business, tell you what we would do and what it would cost, and you decide. If it doesn't make sense for you, we'll tell you that too.

Can I pay in US dollars?

Yes. You can pay in US dollars or in Mexican pesos, by bank transfer. Your plan states the currency and amount in writing.
